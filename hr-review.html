HR Audit & Review for Nigerian businesses

When HR systems have developed in pieces, it can be difficult to see what is sound, what is inconsistent and what needs attention first. FormWork reviews the documentation, processes and people systems already in place, identifies material gaps and turns the findings into a practical order of priority.

When an HR review is useful

An HR review is useful when the business has grown faster than its HR systems; policies and actual practice no longer match; managers handle similar situations differently; documents have accumulated without clear ownership; recurring people problems suggest a deeper process gap; or leadership wants to understand the current position before investing in new HR systems.

What the review can examine

  • Employment documentation and policies
  • Onboarding and employee records
  • Roles and organisational structure
  • Probation and performance processes
  • Employee-lifecycle practices
  • Management responsibilities
  • How key HR activities are owned and applied

The scope is agreed around the organisation’s circumstances.

How the work proceeds

  1. 1Define the scope
  2. 2Review what exists
  3. 3Understand actual practice
  4. 4Identify and prioritise gaps
  5. 5Set the next actions

What you leave with

Deliverables depend on the agreed scope and are stated precisely in the engagement proposal. A typical review can include a findings summary, prioritised issues, recommended actions and an implementation roadmap.
