HR Consulting

HR consulting for the systems behind how people work.

FormWork works with Nigerian businesses on the HR structures, processes and management systems that make employment, accountability and performance more consistent.

Some engagements begin with a specific requirement. Others begin because several symptoms are appearing at once and the organisation needs to understand what is causing them. The right starting point depends on what is already known.

Not sure what the organisation needs? Start with a review.

An HR Audit & Review gives the organisation a structured view of its current HR documentation, practices and people systems, then identifies what should be addressed first.
