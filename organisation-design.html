Organisation Design & Role Clarity

As organisations change, responsibilities can spread across roles without anyone deliberately redesigning how the work fits together. The result can be duplicated effort, unclear reporting, slow decisions, accountability gaps and unnecessary dependence on senior leaders.

FormWork helps organisations clarify structure, roles and accountabilities around the work the business actually needs done.

Discuss Your Organisation Structure

Structure should follow the work

An organisation chart is only useful when it reflects how responsibility and decision-making need to operate. FormWork begins with the operating context, then works through the relationships between teams, roles, reporting lines and accountabilities.

The work can include

  • Organisation structures and reporting relationships
  • Role purpose and key accountabilities
  • Job descriptions
  • Responsibility boundaries
  • Decision ownership
  • Role interfaces
  • Workforce-structure considerations
  • Implications of a redesign for existing or future roles

Typical situations

  • The business has grown around individuals rather than clearly designed roles.
  • Similar responsibilities sit in several places.
  • Managers are unclear about their authority.
  • Teams repeatedly escalate routine decisions.
  • Job descriptions no longer reflect actual work.
  • A reorganisation is planned but leadership needs a clearer design rationale before moving boxes on an organisation chart.

The outcome

The goal is a structure people can understand and operate: clearer relationships, clearer roles and a more deliberate connection between business needs and how work is organised.
