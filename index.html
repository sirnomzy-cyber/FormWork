When the organisation has changed

People systems do not always keep pace with the business.

Growth, new managers, changing roles and a larger workforce can expose systems that once worked informally. Employment documentation becomes inconsistent. Responsibilities overlap. Decisions return to the same few people. Performance expectations are unclear. HR spends too much time resolving issues that should already have a process. FormWork works with organisations to identify what is not working, decide what needs to change and put practical people systems in place.

A practical starting point

Start by understanding what is already in place.

When the problem crosses several parts of HR, adding another policy or template can treat the symptom rather than the underlying issue. An HR Audit & Review examines the current documentation, processes and working practices, identifies material gaps and gives the organisation a clearer order of priority.
