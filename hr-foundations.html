HR Consulting

HR Foundations & Compliance

Employment becomes harder to manage consistently when contracts, policies, onboarding, employee records and everyday processes have developed separately. FormWork helps organisations put clearer HR foundations in place and make them workable in day-to-day practice.

Discuss Your HR Foundations

Build a more consistent employment framework

The aim is not to accumulate policies. It is to establish the core documentation and processes employees, managers and HR can rely on, with clear ownership and a sensible relationship between what the documents say and what happens in practice.

Areas the work can cover

  • Employment-documentation structure
  • HR policy architecture
  • Employee handbooks
  • Onboarding and records
  • Leave and time-related processes
  • Conduct, grievance and disciplinary workflows
  • Employee changes and exits
  • Process ownership
  • Document control

Where legal interpretation or legal drafting is required, FormWork distinguishes its HR consulting role from advice reserved for qualified legal counsel.

How FormWork approaches the work

1
Review the current position
2
Design the foundation
3
Prepare for use

This service is appropriate when

The organisation is formalising HR for the first time; documents have been created at different stages and no longer fit together; onboarding and records are inconsistent; managers need clearer policy guidance; or growth has made informal HR practices difficult to sustain.
