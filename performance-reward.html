Performance & Reward

Performance systems become difficult to trust when expectations are unclear, reviews happen inconsistently or pay and progression decisions feel disconnected from contribution and role requirements.

FormWork helps organisations build practical approaches to performance, probation, goals, progression and reward that managers can actually use.

Discuss Your Performance Systems

Make performance expectations easier to manage

A good performance system should help managers and employees understand what is expected, how progress is discussed, how decisions are made and what happens when performance is above or below expectations. It should not create a paperwork exercise that only comes alive at review time.

The work can include

  • Performance-management frameworks and cycles
  • Goal and KPI structures
  • Probation processes
  • Manager guidance
  • Review documentation
  • Progression principles
  • Reward frameworks
  • Salary structures or pay bands, where properly scoped and supported by suitable data

Typical situations

  • Managers set goals differently.
  • Probation decisions are inconsistent.
  • Annual reviews exist but have little effect on day-to-day management.
  • Employees do not understand how progression works.
  • Salary decisions have developed case by case.
  • Leadership needs a clearer framework without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.
